Emakulator Channel Tools

Private desktop tools used only by their owner to manage his own three YouTube channels (Billion-Dollar Failures, Phantom Megaprojects, How Big Things Work).

What the tools do

  • Studio Setup — fills in the details of videos the owner uploaded to his own channels: titles, descriptions, translated titles/descriptions, video language, playlists, "made for kids" and altered/synthetic-content disclosure, and the publish schedule. It reads every change back to verify it.
  • Channel Stats — a read-only daily report of the owner's own channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers per video and language).

The tools are not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else. They sign in with the owner's Google account through OAuth 2.0 and only access the owner's own channels.
