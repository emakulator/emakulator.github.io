Privacy policy

Emakulator Channel Tools is used only by its owner, for his own YouTube channels.

With the owner's permission it reads YouTube channel data and analytics, and updates details of the owner's own videos through the YouTube Data API. Sign-in tokens and data stay on the owner's own computers and Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
